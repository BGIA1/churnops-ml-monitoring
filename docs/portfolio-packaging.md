# Portfolio Packaging

Lead reviewers through the lifecycle rather than the algorithms: run the demo, inspect the
registry, show a gate decision, call the API, and open drift/performance reports. Explain why
local-first reduces setup risk and why cloud templates are deliberately non-applied.

Avoid claims of production traffic, business lift, or cloud deployment. The strongest evidence
is executable engineering: tests, deterministic outputs, immutable artifacts, failure paths,
security controls, and documented limitations.
